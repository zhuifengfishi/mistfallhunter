## Quick Recommendation

There is no permanent strongest class. Start with the weapon rhythm and team role you can execute reliably, then reassess after balance updates.

## Best Class for Beginners

Mercenary is a sensible first comparison point because a frontline plan makes positioning and retreat choices easy to read. If ranged spacing feels more natural, try Blackarrow instead. Pick the class whose basic loop you can repeat under pressure.

## Best Class for Solo Extraction

Solo runs reward safe exits, route awareness, and the discipline to disengage. Shadowstrix can suit players who enjoy choosing short engagements, while Withered Knight can suit players who prefer durable melee pacing. Neither replaces learning when to leave with your loot.

## PvE Recommendation

For PvE, bring the role your group lacks rather than chasing a universal ranking. Mercenary and Withered Knight can hold space, Seer can support ranged control, and Blasphemer can pressure nearby targets. Treat this as a role checklist, not a tier list.

## PvP Recommendation

PvP outcomes turn on timing, sight lines, and coordination. Blackarrow rewards deliberate precision, Shadowstrix rewards decisive flanks, and Seer rewards setup and support. Practice one response to a missed opening before judging a class.

## Team Roles

Reliable teams usually cover space, pressure, and recovery. Mercenary and Withered Knight can anchor melee space; Seer can reinforce a ranged-support plan; Blackarrow creates precision pressure; Shadowstrix looks for flanks; and Blasphemer brings a bruiser approach. Communicate the job before the fight begins.

## Class Selection Flow

1. Choose melee, ranged, or mixed-distance comfort first.
2. Decide whether you want to anchor, support, pressure, or flank.
3. Run several low-stakes matches with one class and note where your plan breaks.
4. Change class only after identifying a repeatable problem, then recheck the current patch notes.

## Methodology and Balance Snapshot

This guide uses a role-and-playstyle comparison, not a permanent power ranking. The snapshot date is 2026-08-09. Balance changes, map conditions, team composition, and player familiarity can all change the practical answer; verify current details through the official game channels before committing to a build.
